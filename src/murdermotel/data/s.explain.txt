{var:highlightcolor}MURDER MOTEL{var:normalcolor} is an on-line C-net game
that was converted to Image V1.0 format, further modified by Mark Dickenson,
ported to Image v3.0 by NISSA, and {var:highlightcolor}ported to bbsengine by
zoidtechhnologies.com{var:normalcolor}.  It allows people on the board to
play against each other rather than against the computer.  The game concept
has elements of the movies PSYCHO and THUNDERDOME based on the game format
of the ASSASSINATION game.  If you've seen these movies and have played
ASSASSINATION I'm sure you'll find {var:highlightcolor}MURDER
MOTEL{var:normalcolor} a blast!
{f6}
When you enter the motel you will be automatically registered.  If you are
already registered and have been killed you will be revived and told who it
was that killed you.  You will be told who your current target is.  This is
the only person you can kill.  When you have killed them you will be
assigned a new person to eliminate.  If you kill everyone you win the game. 
(Unlikely because the other players can revive themselves so it will be rare
for only one person to be left.)
{f6}
In order to kill anyone you must build weapons from the parts strewn all
over the motel.  It takes two pieces to make a weapon.  For example an axe
handle and an axe blade.
{f6}
There are also defenses you can pick up.  They come complete but you can't
carry more than two at once.  If you attack a player that has the defense
for the weapon you use on him, you will be thrown into a random room as a
penalty.  Oh and by the way someone in the game is looking for YOU!  So be
careful who you trust!
{f6}
Odds and ends about the game----
{f6}
At the time of this writing the motel has 140 rooms (i.e.  places) that you
can go.  You will be allowed 60 moves and there can be up to 30 people
playing at once.  (These figures are subject to change.)
{f6}
There are five weapon types and each weapon has a safety that will protect a
player from the use of that weapon.  It works out as follows
{f6}
{var:highlightcolor}WEAPON COMPONENTS        SAFETY{f6}
{white}-----------------        ------{f6}
{var:normalcolor}Axe blade and axe handle   Riot shield{f6}
Pistol and bullets       Flak jacket{f6}
Chlorine and gas sprayer   Gas mask{f6}
Flame thrower and fuel     Extinguisher{f6}
Acid and acid sprayer      Lye{f6}
{f6}
Each weapon can be used indefinitely when it is complete.  You can leave
weapons anywhere even illogical places.  It is possible for one room to
contain all the items, there is no limit to the space available.  There are
six of each weapon that can be assembled as well as six of each defense.
{f6}
Total items 90.
{f6}
{strike}From time to time the program will pause for about 1-30 seconds.  This should only happen once or twice during the game.  This is because the computer needs to do a garbage collection and is not the fault of the game.  Don't get frustrated at these stops you WILL get a chance to move if you are patient!{/all}
{f6}

If you choose option 'M' at the menu you can look at maps of the motel
layout.  These come in very handy.  Be sure to buffer them and print them
out so you'll have them for quick reference later.  There are 4 floors to
the motel plus an extensive underground basement.  {f6}

The software enforces virtually everything.  The software will not allow you
to call several times consecutively and stack all your allowable moves
together.  You may play ONLY if the previous caller was someone else.  Even
if you didn't play you must let someone else log on before the game will
accept you.  You are also, allowed to play only TWO times during any day. 
While online you can enter the motel as many times as you would like until
you use all of your moves.  But you may only enter the game with a FULL 60
moves twice in any one day.  This is to prevent any one person from hogging
the game.  {f6}

The MOTEL NEWS (option P) will allow you to track the progress of all the
players in the game.  It will give a running commentary of who was killed
and by whom, who defended themselves, who registered or was revived, who was
hit by the {var:highlightcolor}THIEF{var:normalcolor} and who found the
TREASURE ROOM.  {f6}

Thief?  What {var:highlightcolor}THIEF{var:normalcolor}?  :grin:  There is
also a {var:highlightcolor}THIEF{var:normalcolor} in the game.  If you enter
his room he will steal some of the items you are carrying.  You will then
have to find new ones.  There is even a chance you will be able to fight him
off and not lose anything.  {f6}

Also new to the game is the {var:highlightcolor}TREASURE
ROOM{var:normalcolor}.  If you find this room you will receive a bonus
number of moves.  This can help greatly when trying to find a cleaverly
hidden person.  Considering the size of the motel there are TWO Treasure
Rooms.  {f6}

The Treasure Rooms and the Thief are randomly placed everytime you enter the
motel.  {f6}

Also you will notice the inclusion of a
{var:highlightcolor}BOOBY-TRAP{var:normalcolor}.  If you find this item you
can drag it along with you.  This is the ONLY time you can carry SIX items. 
If you leave the TRAP in a room with someone in it, then there is a 90%
chance it will go off when they re-enter the motel.  If it does go off then
15 to 30 moves will be removed for that logon and the trap will move to a
random room location.  BEWARE, if you are carrying the trap when you leave
the motel, it will SPRING ON YOU when you re-enter, unless someone moves it
from the room.  So drop it in another room before you leave.  {f6}

Last but not least, when you kill your target you will also get a SMALL
bonus number of moves.  This is to aid you in finding a new place to hide
when you kill your target and are down to 5 moves.  {f6}

Murder Motel Update 3/16/89{f6:1}
---------------------------
{f6}
If you accidentally press the Q key you are no longer immediately returned
to the Motel Main Menu.  You will be queried as to whether or not you really
wish to leave.  {f6}
{strike}You can now use the command stacking feature of the BBS on ALL
commands while playing Murder Motel.  For moves: N^E^E^N (executes a move
NORTH, EAST, EAST and then NORTH) For Dropping things: L^Y^N^N (selects
LEAVE and drops the FIRST item while keeping the second and third).  Or
getting a group of items: T^3^T^55 (picks up item 3 and 55).  These commands
can be stacked in ANY order.  But BEWARE you cannot stop the sequence after
you press RETURN.{/strike}

Murder Motel Update 5/20/89{f6}
---------------------------{f6}
{f6}

There is now a Rabid Wolf roaming the corridors of the Motel.  If you run
into the wolf you had better have a COMPLETE weapon.  If you don't then the
wolf will attack you and remove a number of moves from you.  You can also
attack the wolf if you have a COMPLETE weapon.  You can remove strength from
the wolf with each attack until you kill him.  You will get points for each
attack and a large bonus if you kill the wolf.  After each attack by you or
the wolf he will run to a random room that is connected to the one you are
in.  You can also IGNORE the wolf and hope he doesn't attack you before he
leaves (unlikely).  Or flee from the room in a random direction.  If you
flee the wolf will run also and MAY follow you!

{var:normalcolor}When you are playing MURDER MOTEL the following commands
are available to you {var:highlightcolor}NORTH{var:normalcolor},
{var:highlightcolor}SOUTH{var:normalcolor},
{var:highlightcolor}EAST{var:normalcolor},
{var:highlightcolor}WEST{var:normalcolor},
{var:highlightcolor}UP{var:normalcolor}, and
{var:highlightcolor}DOWN{var:normalcolor} The directional commands will move
your player towards whatever exits are available.  You can only go through
THESE exits; there are no secret doors in the game.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}TAKE        (T){var:normalcolor} - This command allows you to take any item (weapon or defense) that you find in the motel. You can carry a maximum of five things including up to two defenses.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}LEAVE       (L){var:normalcolor} - When you want to drop an object that you are carrying (to make more room for something you need) use this command. A prompt will appear for each object you are  carrying asking in (Y-N) format which you will leave behind.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}KILL        (K){var:normalcolor} - Use this to kill the player you are after. This command is useless unless you are in the same room as your opponent.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}QUIT        (Q){var:normalcolor} - To quit the game and return to the MAIN area. You may go back to the game on the same call if you had any moves left when you quit.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}INFORMATION (I){var:normalcolor} - This will tell you what you are carrying who you must kill what room you are in and what objects or people are in the room with you. You should always check INFORMATION when you begin the game just in case your target has been changed from the last time you called.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}MAP         (M){var:normalcolor} - This takes one movement point and will display any of the five levels.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}REPORT      (R){var:normalcolor} - This will allow you to add a one line message of 76 characters to the Motel Newspaper. This will take one movement point and is great for leading people around in circles.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}HIGH KILLS{var:normalcolor} - This will show the list of players in order of the most kills.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}HIGH POINTS{var:normalcolor} - This will show the list of players in order of the most points.
{f6}
{var:optioncolor}READ PAPER (P){var:normalcolor} - This will allow you to read the Motel Newspaper.  This will contain all information about what has happened in the game, including reports by other players.
