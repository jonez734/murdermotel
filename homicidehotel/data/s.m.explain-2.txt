\cm  When you are playing MURDER MOTEL
the followin commands are available to
you\cn NORTH SOUTH EAST WEST UP DOWN

The directional commands will move
your player towards whatever exits
are available. You can only go
through THESE exits; there are no
secret doors in the game.

\coTAKE\cm - This command allows you
to take any item (weapon or defense)
that you find in the motel. You can
carry a maximum of five things
including up to two defenses.

\coLEAVE\cm - When you want to drop
an object that you are carrying (to
make more room for something you
need) use this command. A prompt
will appear for each object you are 
carrying asking in (Y-N) format
which you will leave behind.

\coKILL\cm - Use this to kill the 
player you are after. This command
is useless unless you are in the
same room as your opponent.

\coQUIT\cm - To quit the game and
return to the MAIN area. You may
go back to the game on the same
call if you had any moves left when
you quit.

\coINFORMATION\cm - This will tell
you what you are carrying who you
must kill what room you are in and
what objects or people are in the 
room with you. You should always
check INFORMATION when you begin the
game just in case your target has
been changed from the last time you
called.

\coMAP\cm - This takes one movement
point and will display any of the
five levels.

\coREPORT\cm - This will allow you
to add a one line message of 76
characters to the Motel Newspaper.
This will take one movement point
and is great for leading people
around in circles.

\coHIGH KILLS\cm - This will show
the list of players in order of
the most kills.

\coHIGH POINTS\cm - This will show
the list of players in order of
the most points.

\coREAD PAPER\cm - This will allow you
to read the Motel Newspaper.  This
will contain all information about
what has happened in the game,
including reports by other players.

\coGET INSTRUCTIONS\cm - This will
give you this instruction file you
are currently viewing.

Pres the '?' to view the appropriate
SINGLE letter command entries for the
above command discriptions.
