;s.m.explain 2 \c1    MURDER MOTEL COMMAND SUMMARY
{lightgray}When you are playing MURDER MOTEL the followin commands are available to you\cn NORTH SOUTH EAST WEST UP DOWN The directional commands will move your player towards whatever exits are available. You can only go through THESE exits; there are no secret doors in the game. 

{darkgreen}TAKE (T){lightgray} - This command allows you to take any item (weapon or defense) that you find in the motel. You can carry a maximum of five things including up to two defenses. 

{darkgreen}LEAVE (L){lightgray} - When you want to drop an object that you are carrying (to make more room for something you need) use this command. A prompt will appear for each object you are  carrying asking in (Y-N) format which you will leave behind.

{darkgreen}KILL (K){lightgray} - Use this to kill the player you are after. This command is useless unless you are in the same room as your opponent. 

{darkgreen}QUIT (Q){lightgray} - To quit the game and return to the MAIN area. You may go back to the game on the same call if you had any moves left when you quit.

{darkgreen}INFORMATION (I){lightgray} - This will tell you what you are carrying who you must kill what room you are in and what objects or people are in the room with you. You should always check INFORMATION when you begin the game just in case your target has been changed from the last time you called.

{darkgreen}MAP (M){lightgray} - This takes one movement point and will display any of the five levels.

{darkgreen}REPORT (R){lightgray} - This will allow you to add a one line message of 76 characters to the Motel Newspaper. This will take one movement point and is great for leading people around in circles.

{darkgreen}HIGH KILLS{lightgray} - This will show the list of players in order of the most kills. 

{darkgreen}HIGH POINTS{lightgray} - This will show the list of players in order of the most points. 

{darkgreen}READ PAPER (P){lightgray} - This will allow you to read the Motel Newspaper.  This will contain all information about what has happened in the game, including reports by other players.
